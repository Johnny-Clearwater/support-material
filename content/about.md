**Support Material** is a biweekly newsletter of under-the-radar open-source
3D printing projects: small repos, fresh commits, few stars.

*Small projects, big overhangs.*

Every other week we dig through recently active repositories tagged with
things like `klipper`, `reprap`, `voron`, `toolchanger` and `non-planar`, and
write up the ones worth your filament.

Subscribe via [RSS](/index.xml). This site uses no trackers, no analytics and
no cookies.
