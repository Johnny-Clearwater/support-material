2. TO TRY - adventurous / niche (rotate 2-3 per issue)
==================================================================
Code forges beyond GitHub
- codeberg.org (search: 3d printer, klipper, reprap)
- gitlab.com (topics: 3d-printing, reprap)
- repos.ecosyste.ms GitHub topic "3d-printing" (sortable by recent push)

Firmware / tooling communities
- klipper.discourse.group (Projects & Mods category)
- plugins.octoprint.org (newest plugins)
- github.com/topics/klipper, /voron, /toolchanger, /non-planar, /reprap
- VoronUsers mods repo (community printer mods)
- forum.duet3d.com (user projects; unusual kinematics live here)
- forum.prusa3d.com (user mods for MK3S - From the Bench candidates)

Open hardware directories & journals
- certification.oshwa.org (newly certified open hardware)
- HardwareX journal (open-access open-hardware papers)
- Journal of Open Hardware
- appropedia.org (RepRap / distributed manufacturing pages)
- kitspace.org (open PCB projects, incl. printer boards)

Maker communities
- hackster.io PROJECTS (not their news section)
- instructables.com (3D Printing contest entries)
- makerfaire.com exhibitor pages (open-source category)
- reprap.org forum
- RatRig, Annex Engineering, and DoomCube community galleries

Fediverse & small social
- lemmy.world/c/3dprinting and other Lemmy 3D printing communities
- Mastodon hashtags: #3DPrinting #RepRap #Klipper (e.g. fosstodon.org)
- Hacker News "Show HN" posts mentioning 3D printing
- r/functionalprint, r/klippers, r/VORONDesign, r/3Dprinting (newest, low-upvote posts)

Personal blogs
- Search "my printer build log" / "I built a printer" on Substack, Medium, and personal sites
